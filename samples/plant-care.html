Repotting Houseplants

Repot when roots circle the bottom of the pot or water runs straight through without soaking in.

Choosing a pot

Move up only one pot size at a time. A much larger pot holds water the roots cannot reach, which brings back the overwatering problem.

  • Terracotta dries quickly and suits plants that hate wet feet.
  • Glazed ceramic and plastic hold moisture longer.

After repotting

Water thoroughly once, then leave the plant out of direct sun for a week while the roots settle.
