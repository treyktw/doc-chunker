# Houseplant Care Guide

A short, made-up guide used to show how the extractor keeps the heading trail.

## Watering

Most houseplants want the top few centimetres of soil to dry out between
waterings. Pushing a finger into the pot is a better guide than a calendar.

### Signs of overwatering

- Yellow lower leaves that feel soft rather than crisp
- Soil that still smells damp a week after watering
- Fungus gnats hovering over the pot

Roots sitting in wet soil cannot take up oxygen, so they rot, and a plant with
rotten roots wilts exactly like a plant that needs water.

## Light

Bright indirect light suits most foliage plants. A south-facing window can
scorch thin leaves in summer.
